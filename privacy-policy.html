Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Shorts-Auto-Pipeline ("we", "our", "the App") collects, uses, and protects information when you authorize the application to access your TikTok, YouTube, or related accounts.

1. Who we are

The App is operated by businessfirstytb. For any privacy-related question, contact us at business.first.ytb@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

When you authorize the App, we may receive the following information through the TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API:

  • Your TikTok user identifier (open ID, union ID)
  • Your basic public profile information (display name, avatar, username)
  • Access tokens and refresh tokens, used solely to perform the actions you authorized
  • Status information about content posted via the App (publish ID, processing status)

The App does not collect your password, your private messages, your followers list, or any other private content from your TikTok account.

3. How we use your information

The information collected is used exclusively to:

  • Authenticate you on the TikTok platform via OAuth 2.0
  • Publish video content to your TikTok account on your behalf
  • Display the publishing status to you
  • Maintain a valid session through token refresh

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, third-party sale, or any purpose unrelated to the operation of the App.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens and minimal account identifiers are stored securely on the workflow automation infrastructure used by the App (n8n) with encryption at rest. Tokens are never shared with third parties. We retain tokens only for as long as necessary to operate the service. You may revoke our access at any time by removing the App from your TikTok security settings.

5. Sharing with third parties

The App does not sell or rent your personal information. The App relies on the following third-party services strictly necessary for its operation:

  • TikTok — to authenticate users and publish videos
  • YouTube (Google) — to publish videos when you authorize this
  • OpenAI, ElevenLabs, Creatomate — to generate the video content the App publishes on your behalf
  • Dropbox, Backblaze — to host the source media used by the App

Each of these services has its own privacy policy and is contacted only with the data strictly required for the requested operation.

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the information we hold about you
  • Request its deletion
  • Withdraw your authorization at any time via your TikTok account settings
  • Lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority (e.g., CNIL in France, EDPB in the EU)

To exercise these rights, contact us at business.first.ytb@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Data retention

We retain your data only for as long as you keep your authorization active. When you revoke access, the associated tokens are deleted within 30 days.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not intended for users under 13 years of age (or the minimum age required by your country). We do not knowingly collect data from minors below this age.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated occasionally. The "Last updated" date at the top of the page reflects the most recent change. Substantial changes will be communicated through the App or by email.
